# Feedback template (optional)

Send this with a review. Free-form replies are fine too; these questions just make feedback easier to file.

---

Thanks for reading the review. Three questions, answer any you like:

1. **Where do you disagree?** Point at a turn or a line in the review.
2. **How would you play it instead?** The line you'd take, and what it gives up.
3. **When is your way better?** Prize count, what the opponent has shown, going first or second, cards in hand.

Anything the review missed about the matchup overall is welcome too.
It helps to know what you play and how long you've played it, so we can weigh the advice.
